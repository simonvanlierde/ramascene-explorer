RaMa-Scene scenario explorer

Interventions on the EXIOBASE 2011 base year, computed by the RaMa-Scene engine. Not a forecast: the engine changes final demand or technical coefficients in the 2011 economy and has no projection step. Three original interventions, plus a material-efficiency grid: five products × five continental regions × three cut sizes, each cell "every industry in the region uses that much less of the product per unit of output".

Provenance (live job)

No precomputed data here yet. The page reads data/results.json (with data/regions.json for the map and data/flows.json for the Sankey), which only a run of the engine on the EXIOBASE dataset produces:

uv run python precompute.py --run --engine ../ramascene-pr2 --data "$DATASETS_DIR" \
    --out data/results.json --out-regions data/regions.json --out-flows data/flows.json

To check the page itself, open it with ?data=tests/fixture.json (synthetic, bannered).
